Why Molly Should NOT Go to the Vet

A formal petition by Molly the Dachshund

Meet Molly

This is Molly. She is a distinguished black dachshund with years of experience in avoiding the vet. Below, she presents her case for why she should absolutely, definitely, 100% NOT go to the vet today (or ever).

🐾 🐾 🐾 🐾 🐾
#1

Look At These Eyes

Just LOOK at these eyes! How could anyone subject these beautiful, soulful, slightly judgmental eyes to the trauma of a vet visit? The sadness. The betrayal. The "I trusted you" energy. Case closed.

#2

Fashion Forward, Not Vet-Ward

Molly is clearly too fashionable for the vet. This rainbow serape sweater was NOT chosen to be covered by a cold stethoscope. She has a reputation to maintain. The vet waiting room is NOT a runway.

#3

Already Perfectly Healthy (Obviously)

Look at this peak physical specimen. Lounging in the shade, conserving energy, supervising humans - these are signs of a healthy dog! Going to the vet might actually CAUSE stress, which is bad for health. Science!

#4

The Blanket Nest Cannot Be Abandoned

It took HOURS to achieve this perfect blanket nest configuration. HOURS! Do you know how long it takes a dachshund to burrow into exactly the right position? The vet can wait. The blankets cannot.

#5

The Cold Table Trauma

The metal table is COLD. Dachshunds are approximately 87% sausage and 13% drama. Cold tables are an affront to sausage dogs everywhere. It's basically a human rights violation. Dog rights? Wiener rights.

#6

She Already Did Her Job Today

Molly has already barked at exactly 3 suspicious leaves, supervised 2 meals, and performed 47 quality control sniffs today. That's a FULL workday. She's earned her rest, not a car ride to the scary place.
